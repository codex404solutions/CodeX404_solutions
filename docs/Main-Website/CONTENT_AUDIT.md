## 1. Brand Positioning & Target Taxonomy

The initial audit found an umbrella "Solutions" navigation grouping. The current working tree follows PRD §7 with the primary order Home, BUILD, GROW, AUTOMATE, Industries, Our Work, Company, Contact, and the Book a Discovery Call CTA. Top-level division links still point to the approved homepage anchors; each dropdown's overview link opens its dedicated division overview route. Existing service routes remain reachable.

The brand architecture has **Three Divisions**:

```text
               CodeX404 Solutions
     "Software & AI that moves your business forward"
                        │
      ┌─────────────────┼─────────────────┐
      │                 │                 │
    BUILD             GROW            AUTOMATE
 (Blue Accent)   (Purple Accent)   (Green Accent)
```

1. **BUILD:**
   - Digital products and software: websites, custom software, e-commerce, SaaS/MVP, CRM/ERP, mobile/PWA, API integrations.
2. **GROW (includes Digital Marketing):**
   - Search Visibility: SEO, AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), Local SEO.
   - Digital Marketing: Multi-channel campaigns, paid social/search, email marketing.
   - Content & Brand Growth: Copywriting, blogs, case studies, digital PR.
   - Conversion & Measurement: Landing pages, CRO, lead generation, analytics & reporting.
   - *Policy:* Only publish active deliverable services; do not guarantee search positions or AI assistant citations.
3. **AUTOMATE:**
   - AI and operational automation: AI assistants, AI agents, RAG systems, workflow automation, CRM & messaging integrations, document processing.

## 2. Claim Corrections from the Initial Homepage Audit

The statements below describe the pre-Phase-2 copy found during the 2026-11-18 audit. Corrections were applied in the current homepage working tree on 2026-11-19. They are retained here as the audit trail; they are not current approved copy.

1. **Zero Bugs:**
   - *Current Text:* "zero bugs", "Deterministic Zero-Defect Guarantee", "Where Bugs Meet Their Match — 100% Quality Assurance".
   - *Correction:* Remove all zero-bug and zero-defect guarantee statements. Replace with descriptions of engineering rigor, automated testing, static analysis, and code reviews.
2. **Uptime & SLA Guarantees:**
   - *Current Text:* "Production Pipeline: 99.99% Availability", "guaranteed SLA uptime".
   - *Correction:* Remove availability SLA guarantees and fixed percentage uptime claims.
3. **Turnaround & Response Times:**
   - *Current Text:* "Response within 24 business hours", "launch in 2 to 4 weeks", "auto-resolved in 1.8s".
   - *Correction:* Remove fixed time guarantees. Rephrase to prompt reviews during business hours without guaranteeing fixed deadlines.
4. **Calculators & Financial Claims:**
   - *Current Text:* "Estimated Annual Capital Saved: $202,500", "Quantify Your Automation & Software ROI".
   - *Correction:* Frame as an illustrative estimation tool with an explicit disclaimer that figures are mathematical models and not guaranteed financial returns.
5. **Client References & IP Ownership:**
   - *Current Text:* "100% IP Ownership / Zero Lock-in".
   - *Correction:* State that ownership, licensing, and handoff terms depend on the written project agreement. Mark showcase items as candidate deliverables found in the project data file.
