## 1. Product vision

CodeX404 Solutions is a technology and digital growth company that helps organisations build digital products, grow their online presence and automate business operations.

The website must communicate these three capabilities clearly:

- **BUILD:** Design and develop websites, applications, software platforms and digital products.
- **GROW:** Improve discoverability, marketing performance, customer acquisition and conversion through digital marketing, SEO, AEO, GEO, content and analytics.
- **AUTOMATE:** Connect systems and automate repetitive work using software, integrations, AI assistants, agents and workflows.

The website is a business development platform. Its main job is to help a prospective customer understand the company, find the right service, assess credibility, view relevant work and submit a qualified enquiry.

## 2. Business goals

1. Present CodeX404 as one coherent company with three clearly differentiated divisions.
2. Generate qualified enquiries from India and international markets.
3. Explain service capabilities without exaggerating experience or promising outcomes that cannot be guaranteed.
4. Showcase verified portfolio projects, demonstrations and case studies.
5. Make it easy for visitors to select BUILD, GROW or AUTOMATE.
6. Establish trust through clear process, ownership terms, security practices and transparent communication.
7. Provide a content structure that can grow without creating thin or duplicate pages.
8. Support search visibility through sound technical SEO and useful content.
9. Provide a maintainable content and enquiry workflow for the company.
10. Preserve the existing brand identity and working functionality wherever practical.

## 3. Success measures

Track these measures after analytics and consent configuration are verified:

- Qualified enquiries by division.
- Contact form completion rate.
- Consultation requests.
- Enquiry-to-meeting rate.
- Meeting-to-proposal rate.
- Proposal-to-win rate, if recorded in the CRM.
- Service page engagement.
- Portfolio and case-study visits.
- Organic impressions, clicks and relevant search queries.
- Landing-page conversion rate.
- Form delivery success and failure rate.
- Mobile usability and Core Web Vitals.

Do not publish internal metrics as public marketing claims unless they are verified and approved.

Potential manageable content:

- Blog/insights.

- Case studies.

## 13. Non-functional requirements

- Responsive from 320px to 1920px.
- Accessible semantic HTML and keyboard navigation.
- Good color contrast.
- Optimised images and fonts.
- Avoid unnecessary animation and layout shift.
- Maintainable components and code.
- Secure form handling.
- Search-friendly rendering and metadata.
- Stable URLs and redirect plan.
- Error handling and useful logs.
- Backups and tested rollback.
- No production deployment without explicit approval.

## 14. SEO, AEO and GEO requirements

SEO:

- Unique page titles and meta descriptions.
- Canonical URLs.
- XML sitemap and robots.txt.
- Crawlable internal links.
- Correct headings.
- Image alt text.
- Redirect mapping.
- Appropriate structured data.
- Search Console and analytics verification.

AEO:

- Clear answers to genuine customer questions.
- Helpful FAQ and how-to content.
- Logical headings and semantic structure.
- Accurate business information.
- Structured data only where eligible and appropriate.

GEO:

- Original, useful, evidence-based content.
- Clear company and author identity.
- Consistent entity information.
- Credible references and case studies.
- No fake citations, fake reviews or artificial mentions.

Do not promise rankings, featured snippets, AI citations or placement in any answer engine.

## 15. Acceptance criteria

The project is acceptable when:

- The three divisions are obvious across the site.
- The approved visual identity is preserved.
- Existing routes and useful features are accounted for.
- Every published page has unique, accurate content.
- GROW includes Digital Marketing as well as SEO/AEO/GEO.
- Enquiries work end-to-end and are protected.
- Mobile layouts work without horizontal overflow.
- SEO metadata and redirects are tested.
- No unsupported public claims remain.
- Security checks pass.
- Staging QA is completed.
- Production deployment is separately approved.

## 16. Out of scope unless separately approved

- Rebuilding the business backend without a demonstrated need.
- Framework or database migration solely for preference.
- New paid third-party integrations.
- Publishing unverified case studies or testimonials.
- Guaranteed SEO, advertising or AI visibility results.
- Launching every possible service page in the first release.
- Production database changes without backup and migration review.
