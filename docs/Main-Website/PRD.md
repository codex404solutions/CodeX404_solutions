# CodeX404 Solutions — Main Website
# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Document Purpose
This document defines the product, content, UX, conversion, technical and quality requirements for the complete redesign of the CodeX404 Solutions public website.

The website is the company's primary global-facing digital presence. It must communicate that CodeX404 Solutions is an India-based but globally capable Software + AI technology partner.

The website must not be designed as an India-only agency website and must not compete primarily on low price.

## 2. Business Objective

### Primary objectives
1. Establish a credible global technology brand.
2. Explain the company's capabilities in simple business language.
3. Attract qualified Indian and international prospects.
4. Convert visitors into discovery calls and qualified enquiries.
5. Showcase software engineering, AI, automation and SaaS capabilities.
6. Build trust despite being a growing company.
7. Create a strong foundation for future CRM integration.
8. Create reusable content structures for future case studies and SaaS products.

### Secondary objectives
- Support founder-led sales.
- Improve LinkedIn-driven traffic conversion.
- Support outbound campaigns.
- Provide a professional URL to prospects.
- Give agency partners a credible technology partner page.
- Create SEO landing pages for high-intent services.

## 3. Strategic Positioning

### Recommended positioning
**AI & Software Engineering Partner for Growing Businesses**

### Core message
**Build. Automate. Scale.**

### Brand promise
CodeX404 helps businesses turn operational problems, manual workflows and software ideas into practical digital systems.

### Do not position the company as
- Cheap Indian developers
- Low-cost outsourcing
- A generic web-design freelancer
- A company claiming expertise in every possible technology
- An AI company that adds AI merely for marketing

## 9. Homepage Requirements

### Hero
Must answer within seconds:
- What does CodeX404 do?
- Who does it help?
- Why is it different?
- What should I do next?

Suggested direction:
**Software & AI that moves your business forward.**

Supporting:
**We design, build and automate digital systems that help growing businesses operate smarter, move faster and scale with confidence.**

CTAs:
- Book a Discovery Call
- Explore Solutions

### Trust/value strip
Show concise capability statements rather than unsupported numbers.

### Problems section
Explain common business problems.

### Solutions section
Map problems to solutions.

### AI section
Show practical AI applications.

### Automation section
Show workflow transformation.

### Software section
Show custom product engineering.

### Industry section
Explain how solutions map to industries without making unsupported expertise claims.

### Case studies
Use real evidence only.

### Process
Discover → Strategize → Design → Build → Test → Launch → Improve.

### Security
Explain engineering and operational practices.

### Final CTA
Use a clear conversion message.

## 13. Content Requirements
Content must:
- be understandable by business decision makers
- avoid excessive jargon
- explain technical terms where needed
- focus on outcomes
- avoid fake claims
- avoid unsupported statistics
- avoid generic AI buzzwords
- use consistent terminology

## 14. SEO Requirements
Each indexable page needs:
- unique title
- unique meta description
- canonical
- Open Graph
- social image
- one H1
- logical H2/H3 structure
- internal links
- descriptive image alt text
- structured data where appropriate
- sitemap inclusion

## 15. Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented practices:
- keyboard navigation
- visible focus
- semantic HTML
- accessible forms
- proper labels
- sufficient contrast
- reduced motion
- meaningful link text
- accessible menus
- accessible dialogs

## 16. Performance
Targets:
- fast first render
- optimized images
- minimal JavaScript
- deferred non-critical scripts
- responsive images
- modern image formats
- limited third-party scripts
- strong Core Web Vitals

## 17. Future CRM Integration
The main website must NOT contain the full CRM.

It should provide a clean integration point:
```text
Website form
   ↓
Validation
   ↓
Secure API/Webhook
   ↓
Future CodeX404 CRM
```

The browser must never contain CRM secrets.

## 18. Acceptance Criteria
The website is accepted when:
- all approved pages exist
- navigation works
- responsive layouts work
- forms work
- SEO fundamentals exist
- accessibility basics pass
- performance is optimized
- no fake claims exist
- no placeholder content remains
- no production errors are visible
- CRM integration point is documented
