## 2. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) & GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

### AEO Strategy:
- Answer engines (Google Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT Search) favor direct, authoritative answers to specific technical questions.
- FAQ content was revised to describe ownership, timing, confidentiality, and support as terms to agree for each project rather than universal guarantees.
- **Recommendation:** Maintain clear FAQ structures on division pages, backed by valid `FAQPage` schema.

### GEO Strategy:
- Generative AI citation systems evaluate entity coherence, technical clarity, and authentic credentials.
- CodeX404 is positioned clearly around the entity `CodeX404 Solutions`, with explicit division capabilities:
  - BUILD: Custom software, websites, SaaS, mobile applications, APIs.
  - GROW: Digital marketing, SEO, AEO, GEO, content strategy, CRO.
  - AUTOMATE: Autonomous AI agents, RAG architectures, workflow automation.
- *Strict Rule:* Never add deceptive hidden schema or fabricate citations. All descriptions must reflect verified engineering capability.
